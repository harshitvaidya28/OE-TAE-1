The Project

Project Overview

A focused brand-awareness campaign designed to take a brand-new café from invisible to unforgettable in its local market.

The Problem

A newly opened café with great coffee but zero local recognition — invisible in search, unknown on social, and competing against established favourites.

The Objective

Build top-of-mind brand awareness within 90 days — growing reach, reviews, footfall and a loyal community, all on a lean startup budget.

The Strategy

A 360° plan: cinematic content, local SEO, multi-platform social, targeted offers, influencer tie-ins and an email loyalty engine.

Expected Result

A recognized neighbourhood brand with strong local search presence, active community, and a repeat-customer base driving growth.

01 — Business Profile

The story behind LUMI CAFÉ

A warm, design-forward café built to connect its neighbourhood — one thoughtful cup at a time.

EST. 2026

LUMI — meaning “light” — is a specialty café that blends slow-brewed craft coffee, seasonal pastries and a serene, sun-lit space designed for gathering, studying and unwinding.

The brand lives on a simple belief: a great café is not just a place to drink coffee, but a place to connect and belong.

Vision

To become the most-loved neighbourhood café — a daily ritual and a community landmark.

Mission

To serve exceptional coffee and craft a warm environment where every guest feels they belong.

03

Marketing Channels

Purpose & what we will do on each platform.

Instagram

Purpose

Build a visually striking brand presence where local coffee lovers discover and follow LUMI.

What we'll do

Post aesthetic photos, Reels, Stories and behind-the-scenes content; engage daily with the local community.

Facebook

Purpose

Reach community members, local groups and an older local audience looking for a café nearby.

What we'll do

Post updates, events, offers and customer photos; answer messages and join local community groups.

Google Business Profile

Purpose

Become the top local café in Google Maps search for nearby hungry & thirsty customers.

What we'll do

Optimise the profile, add photos & menu, reply to reviews, and post weekly updates to boost visibility.

Local SEO

Purpose

Rank for local searches like "café near me" and capture high-intent nearby customers.

What we'll do

Optimise listings across directories, keep NAP consistent, and create a local landing page with keywords.

Influencer Marketing

Purpose

Leverage trusted local creators to build buzz, credibility and reach fast in the community.

What we'll do

Partner with local foodie & lifestyle influencers for visits, Reels and Stories to amplify reach.

Paid Advertising

Purpose

Accelerate reach and footfall with targeted local ads during the launch window.

What we'll do

Run geo-targeted Instagram & Facebook ads and Google local campaigns around our neighbourhood.

Email Marketing

Purpose

Turn one-time visitors into loyal regulars with personalised offers straight to their inbox.

What we'll do

Build our list with a WiFi/order lead magnet and send welcome, birthday and seasonal campaigns.

Local / Offline

Purpose

Meet the local community face-to-face and put LUMI on the map where people actually gather.

What we'll do

Distribute posters & QR codes, attend local markets and partner with neighbourhood shops & schools.
