# ☕ CUP OF CHAI - Marketing Portfolio

A premium, clean, and professional single-page **Marketing Portfolio & Business Strategy** for **Cup of Chai** cafe. Built strictly in a single standalone file using HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Business Profile**: Brand overview, ethos, 4 key highlight metrics, and ambient cafe imagery.
2. **Target Audience Personas**: 5 customer segments (Students, Young Professionals, Couples & Friends, Families, and Tea Connoisseurs).
3. **SEO Keywords Strategy**: Exactly 10 keywords (5 short-tail & 5 long-tail) with search intent, monthly volume, and placement strategy.
4. **Social Media Marketing Plan**: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, and Google Business Profile with interactive single-post modal previews.
5. **Advertisement Design**: High-converting digital promotional advertisement creative with promo code `CHAI50`.
6. **Email Marketing**: Realistic email client preview featuring welcome offer, promo code `WELCOME20`, and primary CTA button.
7. **Business Improvement Roadmap**: 8 actionable growth strategy cards.
